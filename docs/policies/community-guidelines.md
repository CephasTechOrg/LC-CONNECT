# LC Connect — Community Guidelines

**Version 2 · Effective: September 15, 2026**

LC Connect is your campus, in an app. These are the rules for how we treat each other here. They
are part of the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md), so breaking them can cost you your
account.

The short version: **treat people the way you would if you were standing in front of them.**

---

## Be decent to people

**Do:**
- Introduce yourself like a person.
- Take "no" for an answer — to a connection request, an invitation, or a conversation.
- Assume the person on the other side is a classmate having a normal day.

**Don't:**
- Harass, threaten, bully or follow someone around the app.
- Attack people for their race, colour, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation,
  national origin, disability, or age.
- Swear at someone or call them names. Strong language in general is your business; aimed at a
  person, it is harassment.
- Keep messaging someone who has stopped replying, or make a new account to get around a block.
- Post sexual content, or make sexual comments or advances that were not invited.
- Share nude or sexual images of anyone. Sharing them of a minor is a crime and will be reported to
  the authorities.

## Respect people's privacy

- **Do not share someone's private information** — their phone number, address, class schedule,
  where they live, or their family details.
- **Do not screenshot or forward someone's messages** outside the conversation without asking.
- **Do not post photos of people who did not agree to it.**
- Groups are semi-public. Anything you post there, every member can see.

Remember that **your own profile is visible to the whole campus community** by default, not just to
people you have connected with. If you would rather it were not, hide it or restrict it to
confirmed members in your settings.

## Be who you say you are

- Use your real name and your own photo.
- **Do not impersonate anyone** — another student, a professor, a staff member, or LC Connect
  itself.
- **Do not sign up with a campus email address that is not yours.** You confirmed it was yours when
  you signed up. It is a small campus and administrators check identities against campus email —
  this gets noticed.
- If you are staff, do not claim a title, department or role you do not hold. Positions are checked
  by an administrator before they appear in the directory.

## Do not use LC Connect to sell, recruit or scam

- No advertising, promotion or commercial solicitation.
- No multi-level marketing, crypto schemes, "easy money" offers, or fake job postings.
- No requests for money, gift cards or payment details.
- Do not use connection requests, group invites or activities as a way to reach people with a pitch.

**If someone asks you for money or payment details, do not send anything.** Report them. No
real opportunity posted here will ever ask you for money in a direct message.

## Keep it real when it matters

- Attendance codes are for you. **Do not share, photograph or relay a check-in code** so someone
  else can be marked present. That is academic dishonesty, and it goes to the instructor.
- Do not file reports you know to be untrue. Reporting is there to keep people safe; abusing it
  wastes the time that protects someone else.

## Meeting up in person

LC Connect will help you find people. It cannot tell you whether they are safe.

Nobody organises, supervises or vets member-created activities, and nothing verifies a person
beyond checking they hold a campus email address.

If you are meeting someone from the app:

- meet somewhere public, on campus where you can;
- tell a friend where you are going and when you will be back;
- arrange your own way there and back;
- and **leave if something feels off.** You do not owe anyone an explanation.

## Look after each other

If you see something wrong, you have two tools and both take one tap:

- **Block** — they disappear from your profile, messages and discovery, and you from theirs. They
  are not told.
- **Report** — send it to an administrator with a reason.

A report is read by an administrator, who sees **a copy of the exact message you reported**
— saved at the moment you reported it, so it survives even if the sender deletes it. Administrators
cannot browse your conversations; they only see what was reported. Reports are not anonymous to
administrators; we record who filed them.

**If someone is in immediate danger, call emergency services first.** LC Connect is not an
emergency service and nobody is monitoring it around the clock.

## What happens if you break these rules

Depending on what happened, we may remove the content, warn you, take away a specific ability, or
**suspend your account**. Serious matters get referred to the College's conduct process.

**If we suspend you, we will tell you in the app and you can appeal right there.** An administrator
reads your appeal and resolves or dismisses it. You will not be locked out silently with no way to
respond.
