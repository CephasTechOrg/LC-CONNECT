# LC Connect — Terms of Service

**Version 1 · Effective: [launch date]**

LC Connect is an app for the Livingstone College campus community. These are the rules for using
it. By creating an account you agree to them, to the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md), and to
the [Community Guidelines](community-guidelines.md).

If you do not accept, you cannot use LC Connect.

> LC Connect is built for the campus community and requires a Livingstone College email address to
> join. It is operated by the team that builds it, not by the College's IT department.

---

## 1. Who can use LC Connect

You need a current Livingstone College email address.

- You sign in with your **campus email** (`@students.livingstone.edu` or `@livingstone.edu`). This
  is your identity here.
- Whether you are treated as a **student** or as **staff** comes from your email domain. You cannot
  choose it.
- You also give a **personal email address**. Every code and security notice goes there, because
  you may not be able to reach your campus mailbox yet.

### What you are confirming when you sign up

Signing up means you confirm two things:

1. **The campus email address is yours.** Not a friend's, not one you found. We send your
   confirmation code to your personal inbox, so the app cannot tell on its own whether a campus
   address really belongs to you — which is exactly why this matters. It is a small campus, and
   administrators verify identities against campus email when they grant the verified badge. Using
   someone else's campus address will be noticed, and it will cost you your account.
2. **The personal email address is yours.** It is where your password resets go. Putting someone
   else's address there means handing them a way into your account.

Your account is yours alone. Do not share it and do not sign in as anyone else.

The check mark on a profile means an administrator confirmed that person's campus identity. It is
not automatic and you cannot grant it to yourself.

## 2. What LC Connect is

A campus community app. You can:

- build a profile and find other students to connect with;
- message people you are connected to;
- create and join groups and activities;
- read campus announcements, opportunities and resources;
- find staff in the campus directory and contact them;
- and, if you are a Presidential Scholar, share a professional profile with approved employers.

We may add, change or remove features. We will tell you about changes that meaningfully affect you.

## 3. The rules

The full behaviour rules are in the [Community Guidelines](community-guidelines.md), and they are
part of this agreement. In short, do not use LC Connect to:

- harass, threaten, bully or intimidate anyone;
- post hate speech, or attack people for their race, colour, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual
  orientation, national origin, disability, or age;
- send sexual content, or make unwanted sexual advances;
- swear at or demean another person;
- impersonate anyone, including staff;
- share someone's private information — their address, phone number, schedule, or their messages —
  without permission;
- spam, advertise, recruit, or run any scheme to take money from people;
- lie about your role, title or department;
- cheat the attendance check-in, including sharing or photographing a code so someone else can be
  marked present;
- or break the law or College policy.

### Using the app as intended

- **Do not try to reach data you were not given.** No accessing other people's accounts, no
  scraping the student or staff directory, no automating requests, no probing the API for
  weaknesses.
- **Respect the limits.** Connection requests, messages, group invites, reports, uploads and
  password resets are all rate-limited. Working around those limits is a violation.
- **If you find a security problem, tell us** at `support@livingstone.edu` instead of using it. We
  will not come after you for reporting something in good faith.

## 4. Meeting people in person

LC Connect helps you find people. It does not vet them.

Nobody organises, supervises or screens activities that members create, and nothing verifies that a
person is who they say they are beyond checking they hold a campus email address. **Meeting anyone
in person is your own decision and your own risk.** Meet in public, tell someone where you are
going, and leave if something feels wrong. If someone makes you feel unsafe, block and report them
— both take one tap.

## 5. Your content

What you post stays yours. By posting it you let us store and display it inside LC Connect so the
app can work — showing your profile to other members, delivering your messages, listing your
activity. That permission ends when you delete the content or your account, except for records we
keep for safety (see the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md)).

You are responsible for what you post. Do not post anything you do not have the right to share.

We may remove content that breaks these rules, without notice when someone's safety is at stake.

## 6. Announcements and opportunities

Announcements are posted by administrators, and by staff with a verified campus position. Urgent
alerts are administrator-only. They are how campus news reaches you in the app, so it is worth
reading them.

Opportunities are posted by administrators and by approved partner organisations. **No opportunity,
employer or outcome is guaranteed**, and we are not responsible for how an outside organisation
treats you. Check things out yourself before sharing personal information with an employer.

## 7. Staff responsibilities

A verified campus position gives you abilities other members do not have: publishing campus posts,
and messaging any member without being connected to them.

Use them for campus business. A position can be revoked, which removes those abilities and closes
staff message threads for both sides.

## 8. If you break the rules

Depending on what happened, we may:

1. remove the content;
2. warn you;
3. revoke a specific ability, such as staff publishing;
4. **suspend your account**; or
5. refer the matter to the College if it belongs in its conduct process.

**If we suspend you, we will tell you in the app, and you can appeal right there.** An
administrator reads the appeal and resolves or dismisses it. You keep enough access to file it —
suspension does not lock you out silently.

Administrators reviewing a report can see the specific message that was reported. They cannot
browse your conversations. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

Filing reports you know to be false is itself a violation.

## 9. Ending your use

You can delete your account at any time from the app. The [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md)
explains exactly what deletion does and what it cannot undo.

We may suspend or close an account that breaks these rules, or when you are no longer at the
College.

## 10. The honest limits

LC Connect is provided **as-is**. We do not promise it will always be available, free of errors, or
free of people behaving badly. We work to keep it secure, but no system is perfectly secure.

We are not responsible for what another member does, or for anything that happens between members
outside the app.

If something goes wrong, contact us. Most things are easier to sort out directly, and anything
that belongs in the College's own conduct or grievance process will go there.

## 11. Changes

We may update these terms. When a change affects your rights or what is expected of you, we will
raise the policy version and ask you to read and accept the new version next time you open the app.
Small corrections get a new effective date without interrupting you.

## 12. Contact

`support@livingstone.edu` — for support, conduct questions, privacy requests, and security reports.

We aim to answer privacy requests within **5 business days**.
