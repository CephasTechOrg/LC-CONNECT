# LC Connect — Employer Agreement

**Version 1 · Effective: [launch date]**

For organisations approved to view scholar profiles and post opportunities through the LC Connect
employer portal.

By using the employer portal, your organisation agrees to this. If you do not accept it, you cannot
access scholar profiles.

---

## 1. What you get access to

Once an administrator approves your organisation, your account can:

- view the professional profiles of Presidential Scholars **who have chosen to be visible to
  employers**; and
- submit opportunities for review. Nothing you submit appears in the app until an administrator
  approves it.

For each visible scholar, you can see exactly this and nothing more:

> display name, professional summary, skills, career interests, LinkedIn and Handshake links,
> headshot, and résumé.

You do **not** get their social profile, bio, major, class year, interests, activities, groups,
messages, or contact details. The portal is built so those cannot reach you even by accident.

## 2. Visibility is the scholar's choice, and it can be withdrawn

Scholar visibility is **off by default**. Each scholar opts in individually, and each can turn it
off at any time.

When a scholar withdraws, **your access to their profile ends immediately**. Anything you already
downloaded or saved is yours to delete — see section 4.

## 3. What you may use the data for

**Recruitment and hiring only.** Specifically: assessing a scholar for a role, internship or
opportunity at your organisation, and contacting them about it.

You may **not**:

- share, sell, publish or pass scholar data to any other organisation, including affiliates,
  parent companies, subsidiaries, or recruiting partners;
- add scholars to a mailing list, CRM, marketing database or applicant pool beyond the role they
  were considered for;
- use scholar data to train or evaluate a machine-learning model;
- use automated tools, scripts or scraping against the portal — browse it as a person would;
- share your portal login, or let anyone outside your organisation use it;
- attempt to reach any data the portal does not show you; or
- contact a scholar about anything other than a genuine opportunity.

## 4. Keeping and deleting what you download

If you download a résumé or headshot, you are responsible for it from that moment on.

- Keep it only as long as the hiring process it relates to.
- **Delete it within 12 months**, or sooner if the scholar withdraws visibility or asks you to.
- Store it somewhere only the people making the hiring decision can reach.
- If you have a breach that exposes scholar data, tell us at `support@livingstone.edu` promptly.

## 5. We log your views

Every time your account views a scholar profile, we record which account viewed which profile and
when.

Scholars can ask for that log. This is not hidden from them, and you should assume any view is
visible to the person you viewed.

## 6. Treat people fairly

- Follow applicable equal-opportunity and non-discrimination law in your hiring.
- Do not ask a scholar for money, payment details, or any fee at any stage.
- Do not misrepresent your organisation, the role, the pay, or the terms.
- Do not use the portal to recruit for anything unlawful, or for unpaid work presented as paid.

## 7. Opportunities you post

Everything you submit is reviewed before it appears. We may reject or remove a posting, and we do
not have to explain why.

What you post must be accurate — a real role, at your organisation, on the terms described.

## 8. If this is broken

We may suspend or remove your organisation's access, without notice where scholar data is at risk,
and remove your postings. Serious misuse may be reported to the College.

## 9. The honest limits

The portal is provided **as-is**. We do not guarantee it will always be available, that any scholar
will respond, or that you will fill any role.

We do not verify scholar-supplied information — summaries, skills and résumés are written by the
scholars themselves. Do your own checks before hiring.

## 10. Ending access

You can ask us to close your organisation's account at any time. We may close it if this agreement
is broken or if your organisation is no longer an approved partner. Closure does not release you
from section 4 — you still have to delete what you downloaded.

## 11. Changes

We may update this agreement. Significant changes raise the version, and you will be asked to
accept the new one before continuing to use the portal.

## 12. Contact

`support@livingstone.edu`

## Notes for us, not for employers

Worth recording while it is fresh — these are gaps between this document and what the portal
currently enforces:

- **Sections 3 and 4 are promises, not controls.** Nothing technically stops an employer saving a
  résumé and keeping it. That is true of every recruiting platform, and a contract is the normal
  answer — but it is the reason view logging (section 5) matters: it is the only part of this we can
  actually verify.
- **Withdrawal already works** — consent is checked on every read, so revoking it cuts off access
  immediately. No cleanup job needed.
- **There is no expiry on employer accounts.** An approved organisation stays approved until an
  administrator revokes it. An annual re-approval would make section 10 meaningful rather than
  theoretical; worth considering if the partner list grows.
- **No per-employer rate limit on profile views.** The portal is browsable at human speed today
  because the partner list is short. If it grows, a view cap would make section 3's no-scraping
  rule enforceable instead of aspirational.
